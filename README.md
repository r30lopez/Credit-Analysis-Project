# Commercial-Credit-Analysis
Purpose: Evaluate a commercial agricultural borrower seeking approximately $6.3M in combined operating and real-estate financing.

Scope of Work

Historical financial statement and cash-flow analysis
Liquidity, leverage, DSCR, and break-even analysis
2026–2028 financial projections
Collateral and guarantor analysis
Credit risk and mitigant assessment
Loan structure, covenant, and approval recommendation

My Contribution
Contributed across all phases of the project, including financial analysis, projections, credit-risk assessment, collateral analysis, written recommendations, and final presentation development.

Included Files

San Joaquin Valley Orchards LLC Credit Analysis Presentation.pdf — concise summary of the analysis and recommendation
Full Credit Case.pdf — detailed written underwriting and supporting analysis

Key Outcome
The team recommended approval with conditions, including a $4.2M real-estate term loan and renewal of a $2.1M operating line of credit, supported by projected repayment capacity, collateral analysis, and financial covenants.
